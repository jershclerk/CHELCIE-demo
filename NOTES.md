## Product strategy and market positioning (Amy, 2026-08-31)

- Amy identified the "Atlas" universe visualization (the constellation explorer in this project) as a key competitive differentiator for future commercial product offerings.
- The target persona is defined by organizations experiencing major disruptive funding events, rather than standard demographic segmentation.
- The platform's value proposition centers on identifying non-obvious funding sources and providing strategic guidance, over standard program management.
